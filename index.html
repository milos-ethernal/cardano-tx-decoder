Free Cardano TX Decoder (CBOR)

Decode Cardano transactions directly in your browser. This Cardano TX decoder parses CBOR and shows inputs, outputs, multi-asset mint/burn, metadata, collateral inputs, reference inputs, required signers, validity range, withdrawals and more.

FAQ

Is it safe? Yes. Decoding happens locally in your browser using WebAssembly.

What formats are supported? Paste raw CBOR hex or JSON containing a cborHex field.

Which fields are decoded? We display a JSON similar to cardano-cli transaction view, including mint, metadata, collateral, and reference inputs when present.
